# Hakeem Oluwafemi Adeniyan

## About the Coiner

Hakeem Oluwafemi Adeniyan is the originator and sole creator of all terminology coinages published on this website. Each Yoruba term presented here was coined by Hakeem on its respective publication date, with the goal of expanding Yoruba's lexical capacity in science, technology, mathematics, and education.

All coinages follow systematic principles of Yoruba morphology and phonology, ensuring they are natural to native speakers while filling modern lexical gaps.

## Coinage Methodology

Every coined word on this site is created using one or more of these methods:

- **Compounding** — Combining existing Yoruba roots
- **Derivation** — Adding prefixes/suffixes to existing words
- **Semantic extension** — Expanding meaning of existing terms
- **Phonetic adaptation** — Yoruba-friendly borrowing
- **Neologistic invention** — Novel formations following Yoruba patterns

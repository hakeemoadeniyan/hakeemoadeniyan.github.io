english_meaning: "Bankruptcy"

date_coined: "2026-06-09"
author: "Hakeem Oluwafemi Adeniyan"
definition: >
  Ìjẹgbárun ni pípàdánù dúkìá ẹni sọ́wọ́ àwọn ayánilówó látàrí àwọn gbèsè tí a kò san. (Bankruptcy is the state of losing property to one's creditors due to unpaid depts.)
formation: >
  Ìjẹgbárun = ìjẹ + igbá + run. Igbá jẹ́ dúkìá tàbí ọjà ẹni.
examples:
  - yoruba: "Ìjẹgbárun pa ọ̀pọ̀lọpọ̀ àtijẹ̀bi rẹ̀ rẹ́, ṣùgbọ́n ó pàdánù ilé rẹ̀."
    english: "Bankruptcy wiped out most of his liabilities, but he lost his house."
  - yoruba: "Ó rí ìjẹgbárun gẹ́gẹ́ bí ibùsádi ìgbẹ̀yìn láti mórí bọ́ lọ́wọ́ àwọn àjẹẹ́lẹ̀ owó ìtọ́jú."
    english: "She saw bankruptcy as a last resort to escape medical bills."

title: "Kíkún"
yoruba_term: "Kíkún"
english_meaning: "Absolute"
subject: "General Knowledge"
date_coined: "2022-06-15"
author: "Hakeem Oluwafemi Adeniyan"
definition: >
  **Kíkún** túmọ̀ sí àpapọ̀, pípé tàbí tí kò ní gbèdéke láti ọwọ́ ohunkóhun. (**Absolute** means total, complete, or not limited by anything.)
formation: >
  A sẹ̀dá **kíkún** nípa ṣíṣe àpètúnpé ọ̀rọ̀-ìṣe **kún**.
examples:
  - yoruba: "Ìdákẹ́ inú ibùyáwèékà náà jẹ́ kíkún."
    english: "The silence in the library was absolute."
  - yoruba: "Mo ní ìfọkàntán kíkún nínú àtilèṣe rẹ."
    english: "I have absolute confidence in your ability."

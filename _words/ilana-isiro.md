definition: >
  Ìlànà ìṣirò jẹ́ ọ̀nà ìgbésẹ̀-nípa-ìgbésẹ̀ tí a máa ń tẹ̀lé láti yanjú ìṣòro kan tàbí láti ṣe iṣẹ́ kan pàtó.
  Nínú ìmọ̀ ẹ̀rọ kọ̀mpútà, ìlànà ìṣirò jẹ́ àwọn ìtọ́sọ́nà tó ṣe kedere tí ẹ̀rọ kọ̀mpútà máa ń tẹ̀lé.
  (An algorithm is a step-by-step procedure followed to solve a problem or perform a specific task.
  In computer science, an algorithm is a set of precise instructions that a computer follows.)
formation: >
  The term is built from two native Yoruba words: **Ìlànà** (procedure, rule, guideline — a well-established Yoruba noun)
  and **ìṣirò** (calculation, computation, mathematics — from the verb *ṣirò*, to calculate).
  Together, **ìlànà ìṣirò** means "a procedure for calculation" — describing the core nature of an algorithm
  entirely within existing Yoruba vocabulary, requiring no phonological borrowing.

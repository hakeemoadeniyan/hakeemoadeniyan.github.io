title: "Ìwọ̀ngbóná kíkún"
yoruba_term: "Ìwọ̀ngbóná kíkún"
english_meaning: "Absolute temperature"
subject: "Science"
date_coined: "2022-06-15"
author: "Hakeem Oluwafemi Adeniyan"
definition: >
  Ìwọ̀ngbóná kikún jẹ́ ìwọ̀ngbóná tí a wọ̀n láti òdo kíkún (−273.15°C tàbí 0 K), níbi tí gbogbo ìṣípò mólékù ti ń dúró. (Absolute temperature is a temperature measured from absolute zero (−273.15°C or 0 K), where all molecular motion stops.)
formation: >
  A kó àwọn ọmọnú àsọyé yìí jọ láti ara **ìwọ̀ngbóná + kíkún**.
examples:
  - yoruba: "A máa ń wọn ìwọ̀ngbóná kíkún ní kẹ́lífìn, kì í ṣe ní ipele sẹ́síọ́ọ̀sì."
    english: "Absolute temperature is measured in kelvins, not degrees Celsius."
  - yoruba: "Sísọ ìwọ̀ngbóná kíkún ẹ̀fúùfù kan di ìlópo méjì máa n sọ òòrìn rẹ̀ náà di ilọ́po méjì."
    english: "Doubling the absolute temperature of a gas also doubles its pressure."

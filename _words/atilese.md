title: "Àtilèṣe"
yoruba_term: "Àtilèṣe"
english_meaning: "Ability"
subject: "General Knowledge"
date_coined: "2023-06-15"
author: "Hakeem Oluwafemi Adeniyan"
definition: >
  **Àtilèṣe** túmọ̀ sí níní agbára, ìmọ̀ọ́ṣe, tàbí ọ̀nà láti sẹ nǹkan. (Ability means having the power, skill, or means to do something.)
formation: >
  A ṣẹ̀dá **àtilèṣe** láti ara àti+lè+ṣe.
examples:
  - yoruba: "Àyẹ̀wò-ráńpẹ́ náà wọn àtilèṣe rẹ nínú ìmọ̀-ìṣirò."
    english: "The test measures your mathematical ability."
  - yoruba: "Ó ní àtilèṣe àdámọ́ láti mú kí awọn ènìyàn ní ìmọ̀lára ìdẹ̀kùn."
    english: "He has the natural ability to make people feel comfortable."
